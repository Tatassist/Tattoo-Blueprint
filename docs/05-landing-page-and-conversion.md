# Landing Page and Conversion System

## Goal

Sell a paid consultation directly. No free discovery call. No giant lead magnet funnel.

Primary conversion event: completed paid booking.

## Page structure

### 1. Hero

**Eyebrow:**

**Headline:**

**Subhead:**
Talk it through with an experienced custom tattoo artist and shop owner. Turn the story, screenshots, and half-formed ideas into a clear direction you can confidently take to your chosen artist.

**Primary CTA:**
Choose Your Consultation

**Microcopy:**
Remote video consultations. Starting at $47.

Secondary trust line:
Your artist still designs the tattoo. This helps you communicate the right idea.

### 2. Problem recognition

Heading:
**You do not need to have it figured out first.**

Three problem cards:

**“I know what it means. I don’t know what it should look like.”**
You have the story, person, memory, or idea. The imagery still feels generic or unclear.

**“I have 30 screenshots and no actual plan.”**
You know what you like when you see it, but explaining the common thread is harder.

**“I’m worried I’ll agree too quickly.”**
Tattoo consultations can be intimidating. You want to know what matters before you are standing in a shop trying to make permanent decisions.

### 3. Founder section / video

Heading:
**A good tattoo consultation is more than asking, “What do you want?”**

Suggested video script outline:
- “I’m Joker. I’ve spent years tattooing custom projects and running an appointment-only shop.”
- “A lot of clients are not bad at having ideas. They are bad at translating those ideas in a high-pressure conversation.”
- “Sometimes I can tell someone is agreeing because they’re shy, not because they actually like the direction.”
- “My job in this service is to slow that part down and draw out what really matters.”
- “I’m not designing over your artist. Your artist should design the tattoo. I’m helping you walk in prepared enough to have a much better conversation with them.”

### 4. How it works

**1. Pick the level of help you need.**
Choose a focused clarity call, a full Tattoo Blueprint, or major-project planning.

**2. Send the messy version.**
Story, screenshots, body photos, rough ideas, worries. You do not need tattoo vocabulary.

**3. Talk it through with Joker.**
This is the important part. Joker asks the questions that identify what actually matters and what should stay flexible.

**4. Take a clear brief to your artist.**
You receive a concise plan that explains your priorities without trying to do your artist’s job for them.

### 9. FAQ

**Will Joker design the tattoo?**
No. Joker helps you clarify the project. Your tattoo artist creates the final design.

**Can I use this if I already have an artist?**
Yes. That is one of the clearest use cases. The artist-facing brief is specifically meant to help you communicate without prescribing their design.

**Can I use this if I do not have an artist yet?**
Yes. The consultation can help clarify what style and portfolio qualities you should be looking for. Artist matching is not included in the normal $99 Blueprint.

**What if I barely know what I want?**
That is fine. The service is specifically designed for incomplete ideas.

**What if I have too many ideas?**
The consultation will help establish hierarchy and identify what can be removed.

**Is this only for people in Las Vegas?**
No. The consultation is remote and can be used before seeing a tattoo artist anywhere.

**Can I upgrade if the $47 call reveals I need more help?**
Yes. Upgrade within seven days and the $47 is credited toward the $99 Blueprint.

**Will my artist be offended?**
The brief is intentionally written not to dictate their artwork. It identifies the client’s intent, priorities, flexibility, and questions. The executing artist keeps final design and technical control.

**Can Joker tell me whether my tattoo is healing correctly?**
No. Medical/healing concerns should be directed to your executing tattoo artist or an appropriate healthcare professional.

## Booking flow

Preferred:
Landing page → tier CTA → Calendly event → payment → confirmation page → intake form.

Requirements:
- payment required to reserve time;
- client receives intake immediately;
- client cannot miss the intake link;
- reminders include “intake required before call”;
- UTMs persist through booking if possible;
- purchase event fires only on confirmed payment, not scheduler open.

## Trust / anti-vibe-coded checklist

Every launch review must test:
- every CTA on desktop and mobile;
- every pricing card goes to the correct tier;
- booking calendar has actual availability;
- payment succeeds;
- confirmation email arrives;
- intake link works;
- sample Blueprint can be opened;
- business name/contact are visible;
- no dead navigation;
- no invented artist network;
- no fake review count;
- no generic stock tattoo photography if real Joker/BDCT assets exist;
- no AI tattoo hero art.

## A/B tests after minimum traffic

Test one variable at a time.
