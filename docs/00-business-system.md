# Tattoo Blueprint by Joker Ink — Operating System v1

## Purpose

Tattoo Blueprint is a remote, client-side tattoo planning service built around Joker Ink's existing edge: experienced tattoo judgment, the ability to draw useful information out of clients, and the ability to translate vague ideas into clear direction without replacing the executing tattoo artist.

The MVP must stay small. The product is the conversation and the judgment. The written brief is the handoff artifact, not the reason the service exists.

## Core promise

**Walk into your tattoo consultation knowing what you mean, what matters, and how to explain it to your artist.**

The service is for people who:
- know they want a tattoo but do not know exactly what they want;
- know what it means but cannot translate that meaning into useful visual direction;
- have too many screenshots and no coherent plan;
- feel nervous, shy, intimidated, or likely to agree too quickly in a tattoo consultation;
- are planning a larger project and need help deciding what belongs, what does not, and what must remain flexible;
- already have an artist but want to arrive better prepared.

The service is not:
- finished tattoo design;
- stencil creation;
- a substitute for the executing artist's design process;
- a guarantee that any artist will accept the project;
- medical advice;
- a way to overrule or attack another tattoo artist.

The $99 Tattoo Blueprint is the core product and should be the visually dominant option on the landing page.

## Internal success rule

Do not add deliverables simply to justify price increases. Price changes come only after evidence that demand, calendar pressure, or customer value supports them.

## Customer journey

1. Client discovers Tattoo Blueprint.
2. Client selects a tier and books a paid call.
3. Client completes the required intake before the session.
4. Joker reviews the intake briefly before the call.
5. Joker conducts the consultation using the interview framework.
6. Call notes/transcript are converted into the appropriate brief template with AI assistance.
7. Joker reviews the brief for accuracy and tone.
8. Deliverable is sent.
9. Client remains in the relationship CRM and receives personal, non-salesy follow-up tied to the actual tattoo process.
10. With permission, finished work becomes a case study and the executing artist is credited.
11. Repeat projects, referrals, artist relationships, and later B2B opportunities grow from the relationship.

## Fulfillment time targets

| Tier | Total founder time target |
|---|---:|
| $47 Clarity | <= 30 minutes |
| $99 Blueprint | <= 60 minutes |
| $250 Major Project | <= 120 minutes |

If actual average fulfillment exceeds these limits, simplify the artifact before changing price.

## Service boundaries

Every customer-facing brief should include this principle:

> Your tattoo artist should still design your tattoo. This planning process is intended to make your goals, priorities, references, and areas of flexibility clearer so your artist has better information to work with.

## Launch philosophy

- No app.
- No public artist marketplace.
- No free discovery call.
- No giant membership at launch.
- No automatic artist referral commissions.
- No fake scarcity.
- No elaborate PDF production.
- No need to automate what has not yet been proven manually.

The first business question is simple: **Will strangers pay $99 to have Joker help them figure out their tattoo?**

## Phase gates

### Gate 1 — Manual validation
Target: first 10 paid clients.

Measure:
- tier purchased;
- acquisition source;
- founder minutes spent;
- client clarity score before and after;
- whether the client shows the brief to an artist;
- artist reaction if known;
- refund/dissatisfaction;
- referral or repeat intent.

### Gate 2 — Cold acquisition validation
Target: at least 10 paid clients who did not already know Joker.

Proceed only if:
- paid traffic produces relevant prospects;
- blended CAC is economically tolerable;
- most buyers choose the $99 tier;
- fulfillment remains inside time targets.

### Gate 3 — Relationship validation
Target: 25–50 completed consultations.

Track:
- repeat consultations;
- referrals;
- finished tattoo photos;
- executing artists contacted;
- artists who say the brief helped;
- artists who send a client back;
- clients who later inquire about getting tattooed by Joker.

### Gate 4 — B2B pilot
Only after multiple artists have independently experienced the client brief.

First B2B offer should be simple:
> Send us the clients who have meaningful ideas but cannot organize or explain them. They pay us directly. We send them back to you better prepared.

### Gate 5 — Membership / recurring access
Do not launch until repeated client behavior shows demand for ongoing structured access.

Potential future model:
- monthly membership;
- annual option at ~20% discount;
- normal human follow-up stays free;
- paid membership covers guaranteed structured access, not ordinary relationship care.

## North-star metrics

1. Paid bookings per 100 qualified landing-page visitors.
2. Percentage choosing the $99 core offer.
3. CAC by channel and intent cluster.
4. Founder minutes per completed order.
5. Client clarity delta (before vs after).
6. Percentage of clients who actually take the brief to an artist.
7. Percentage who send a tattoo/progress photo later.
8. Referral rate.
9. Repeat purchase rate.
10. Number of new artist relationships generated from completed client projects.
