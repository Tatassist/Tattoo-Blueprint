# Client Forms

This file contains the exact client-facing forms for the three launch offers.

The forms should feel conversational, not clinical. Ask only what helps the session. Do not force clients to know tattoo terminology.

---

# A. Tattoo Clarity Call Intake — $47

## Intro copy

**You do not need to have the tattoo figured out before this call.**

Give Joker whatever you have right now: the story, the half-formed idea, screenshots, a placement you are considering, or simply the part you are stuck on. The goal is to spend the call solving one focused tattoo-planning problem.

## Fields

### Contact
- Full name [required]
- Email [required]
- Phone [optional]
- City / country [required]
- Instagram handle [optional]

### Your question
1. **What are you trying to figure out?** [long text, required]
   - Helper text: “Write it however you would explain it to a friend. You do not need tattoo terminology.”

2. **What do you already know for sure?** [long text, optional]

3. **What are you unsure about?** [long text, required]

4. **What does this tattoo mean to you, if anything?** [long text, optional]

5. **Where are you thinking about putting it?** [short text, optional]

6. **Do you already have a tattoo artist in mind?** [radio]
   - Yes, already booked
   - Yes, not booked yet
   - I am considering artists
   - No

7. **Upload anything useful.** [file upload, optional, max 10 files]
   - reference images
   - photo of the body area
   - existing tattoo
   - sketch
   - screenshot

8. **If you uploaded references, what do you like or dislike about them?** [long text, optional]

9. **At the end of this call, what would make you say “that helped”?** [long text, required]

### Acknowledgments
Checkboxes [required]:
- I understand this is tattoo planning/consultation, not a finished tattoo design or stencil.
- I understand my chosen tattoo artist makes the final design and technical decisions.
- I understand medical or healing questions should be directed to a qualified healthcare professional and/or my executing tattoo artist as appropriate.

# B. Tattoo Blueprint Intake — $99

## Intro copy

**Bring the messy version.**

You can know exactly what the tattoo means and still have no idea how to explain it visually. You can also have 30 screenshots and still not have a clear plan. Both are normal.

Answer in your own words. Joker will use the call to draw out the parts that are still unclear.

## Section 1 — Contact
- Full name [required]
- Email [required]
- Phone [optional]
- City / state / country [required]
- Time zone [required]
- Instagram / social handle [optional]
- How did you hear about Tattoo Blueprint? [required]
  - ChatGPT
  - Instagram
  - TikTok
  - Google
  - Existing Joker/BDCT client
  - Friend/referral
  - Tattoo artist referral
  - Other

## Section 2 — The project
1. **Tell Joker about the tattoo you are thinking about.** [long text, required]
   - Helper: “Do not worry about organizing it. Tell the story first.”

2. **What is this tattoo supposed to mean, represent, remember, or make you feel?** [long text, optional but recommended]

3. **Which statement feels closest to where you are right now?** [single select, required]
   - I know the meaning but not the imagery.
   - I have imagery but it does not feel organized.
   - I have too many ideas and need to narrow them down.
   - I mostly know what I want but struggle to explain it.
   - I am nervous I will agree to something I do not actually want.
   - I am unsure about placement/size.
   - I want a second set of experienced eyes before I talk to my artist.
   - Other.

4. **What parts already feel non-negotiable?** [long text, optional]

5. **What parts are you open to changing?** [long text, optional]

6. **What would make the tattoo feel wrong, generic, cheesy, too busy, or unlike you?** [long text, required]

7. **Do you want the meaning to be obvious to other people, or can it be personal/private?** [single select]
   - Obvious
   - Mostly readable but not literal
   - Personal/private symbolism is fine
   - I am not sure

## Section 3 — Placement and body context
8. **Where do you want the tattoo?** [short text, required]

9. **How large are you imagining it?** [single select]
   - Small: under ~4 inches
   - Medium: ~4–8 inches
   - Large: ~8–14 inches
   - Very large / multiple body zones
   - I am not sure

10. **Are you open to going larger or changing placement if the idea needs more room?** [single select]
   - Yes
   - Maybe, if there is a good reason
   - No

11. **Upload a clear straight-on photo of the area.** [file upload, required when relevant]

12. **Upload one photo from farther back showing the whole body area and nearby tattoos.** [file upload, required when relevant]

13. **Are there existing tattoos, scars, brands, stretch marks, or other constraints in/near the area?** [long text, optional]
   - If yes, upload clear photos.

## Section 4 — References and style
14. **Upload any references you have saved.** [file upload, optional, max 15]

15. **For each reference, what specifically do you like?** [long text, optional]
   - Helper: “Examples: contrast, mood, pose, composition, realism level, negative space, linework, color, subject. ‘I just like it’ is also okay.”

16. **Anything in your references you definitely do NOT want copied?** [long text, optional]

17. **Are there tattoo styles you are drawn to?** [multi-select]
   - Black and grey realism
   - Color realism
   - Illustrative
   - Neo-traditional
   - Traditional
   - Fine line
   - Blackwork
   - Japanese
   - Lettering
   - Surrealism
   - Geometric / ornamental
   - I do not know
   - Other

18. **Describe the feeling you want the finished tattoo to have.** [long text, optional]
   - Examples are not prefilled in the field; do not lead the client unnecessarily.

## Section 5 — The artist situation
19. **Do you already have an artist?** [required]
   - Yes, booked
   - Yes, talking to them
   - I have a shortlist
   - No

20. If yes: **What drew you to that artist's work?** [long text]

21. If yes: **Is there anything you are nervous about communicating to them?** [long text]

22. If no: **What matters most when choosing the artist?** [multi-select]
   - Style/portfolio
   - Experience
   - Communication
   - Location
   - Price
   - Availability
   - Large-project experience
   - Cover-up experience
   - Other

## Section 6 — Practical context
23. **Is there a target timeline?** [short text, optional]

24. **What budget range are you expecting for the actual tattoo?** [optional, single select]
   - Under $500
   - $500–$1,000
   - $1,000–$2,500
   - $2,500–$5,000
   - $5,000–$10,000
   - $10,000+
   - I have no idea
   - Prefer not to say

Helper text:
> “This does not change the price of your Blueprint. It helps Joker understand whether the idea and likely project size are aligned with your expectations.”

25. **What is the biggest thing you want to leave the call clear about?** [long text, required]

## Permissions / acknowledgments
Required:
- I understand Joker is not creating the final tattoo design or stencil.
- I understand my executing artist decides final tattooability, composition, price, scheduling, and execution.
- I understand suggestions may change once my tattoo artist evaluates the body area in person.

Separate optional permissions, never bundled:
- I consent to the consultation being recorded/transcribed for note-taking and creation of my Blueprint.
- I would be open to being contacted later about sharing my finished tattoo as a case study. This does not grant permission to publish anything.

# C. Major Project Blueprint Intake — $250

Use all $99 questions plus the following.

## Project map
1. **Which body areas are included in this project?** [long text]

2. **Is the goal one continuous composition or several connected pieces?** [single select]
   - One continuous composition
   - Several connected pieces
   - Not sure

3. **List every subject, symbol, person, object, scene, or text element you are considering.** [long text]

4. **If Joker forced you to keep only three of those elements, which three survive? Why?** [long text, required]

5. **Which part should someone notice first from across the room?** [long text, optional]

6. **Are there areas you intentionally want to preserve for future tattoos?** [long text, optional]

7. **Upload photos showing the entire project area from all useful angles.** [required]

8. **Upload close photos of existing work that must be integrated, covered, or worked around.** [conditional]

9. **Are you willing to complete the project over multiple sessions/months?** [single select]
   - Yes
   - Maybe
   - No / I need to discuss this

10. **What would make the overall project feel cohesive to you?** [long text]

11. **What are you worried could make it look like a collection of unrelated stickers/images?** [long text, optional]

12. **Do you want one artist to execute the whole project?** [single select]
   - Yes
   - Probably
   - Open to multiple specialists
   - Not sure

13. **What future tattoo ideas should this project leave room for?** [long text, optional]

# D. Post-consultation client feedback form

Send 24 hours after delivery.

1. Before the consultation, how clear were you on what you wanted? [1–10]
2. After the consultation, how clear are you now? [1–10]
3. What changed most for you? [long text]
4. Is there anything in the Blueprint that does not sound like you or misrepresents what you meant? [long text]
5. Do you already plan to show the brief to a tattoo artist? [Yes / No / Already did]
6. Would you recommend this conversation to someone who was stuck on a tattoo idea? [0–10]
7. Optional testimonial [long text]
8. May Joker quote your testimonial publicly? [Yes / No]

# E. Artist feedback form

This is NOT part of the client's purchase. It is a relationship-building loop.

Intro:
> “Your client worked through their project with Tattoo Blueprint before bringing it to you. The intention is to make their priorities clearer without prescribing your design. A few seconds of blunt feedback helps us make these briefs more useful to working tattooers.”

1. Did the brief make the client's goals easier to understand? [1–5]
2. Did anything feel over-specified? [long text]
3. Was anything important missing? [long text]
4. Did this save time in your consultation? [Yes / No / Not sure]
5. Would you be comfortable receiving another client with a brief in this format? [Yes / No / Maybe]
6. Would you ever send a client to Joker specifically to help them organize an unclear project before your consultation? [Yes / No / Maybe]
7. Artist name / studio / city [optional]
8. Best contact [optional]
